DW-01 Archive Missing Numbers

{{ page.title }}

This is a collection of cards that have some record of being in circulation but for one reason or another we're not able to add it's number to the archive.

  1. This YouTube video is of someone's Digi-Battle card collection and at the timestamp shown there is a numbered DW-01. However the video is very blurry and I can't make out the number. The poster of the video sold the cards years ago so I don't think there's a way to find out what the number is.
  2. Another YouTube collection video with a DW-01 that's too blurry to see. I have contacted the poster and asked if he would submit the number to the site but he hasn't responded.
  3. Indistinguishable number on Instagram.
  4. There is a sealed and graded copy of Digimon World 1 which contains the DW-01 card. Because it's sealed we can't know what the card number is without opening it. This was on ebay for a while but now I can't find an image of it anywhere.
