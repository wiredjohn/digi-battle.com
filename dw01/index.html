What is a DW-01?

A special promo card was distributed with the initial release of Digimon World for the PS1 in North America. That card was a holographic MetalGreymon, limited to 100,000 copies with each card being individually serialized.

You can learn more about it in the Digimon World MetalGreymon Promo article.

What is this website?

This is an attempt to catalog as many DW-01 MetalGreymon promo cards that still exist. The archive stores a picture of the card, its unique number, PSA grade and if possible who currently owns it.

How do I submit my card?

Check out the GitHub Submit Your DW-01 page for the latest info on how to add your card to the site!
