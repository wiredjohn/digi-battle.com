I love the original Digimon Digi-Battle card game. I first thought of building a site like this back in late 2019, I was always flipping through pages in my card folder looking at card art, comparing stats and reading their descriptions. It was during this time I thought it would be cool (and more convenient) to do all that digitally.

I know the Digi-Battle card game gets a lot of criticism, especially when compared to Japan's Hyper Colosseum Digimon Card Game, but Digi-Battle will always be special to me. This website is for everyone to look back and remember a game that's brought joy to many people over the years.

I hope you enjoy!
