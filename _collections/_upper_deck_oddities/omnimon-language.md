Omnimon is the only Digi-Battle card to be printed in Japanese but it was actually part of a toy release in the USA, not Japan. The Japanese name literally translates to "Omnimon", which is interesting because the character's Japanese name translates to "Omegamon" - making it one of the few appearances of the English name appearing in Japanese.
