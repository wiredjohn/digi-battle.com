This isn't an official Digi-Battle set, it's just a collection of the other miscellaneous promotional cards from 1-off releases. Cards included in this category are:

- **MetalGreymon (Dw-01)**: Digimon World 1 promo for the PS1. Was included in the game case for the initial black-label release. I've written up a more [in-depth article about this card](/articles/dw01) on this site.
- **Omnimon (Dm-01)**: Toy Promo, cool thing about this card is it's in Japanese and is the only Digi-Battle card in Japanese despite being released in the USA.
- **The Miracle 4 (Dm-02)**: Toy Promo
- **Goldramon (Un-01)**: Universal Studios event promo. I've written up a more [in-depth article about this card](/articles/un01) on this site.
