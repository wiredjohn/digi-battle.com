The Swedish Promo set was released exclusively in Sweden (fancy that) but thankfully the cards were printed in English. The set features a selection of cards translated from the French Mega Pack along with the Digi-Destined power option cards.

A YouTuber and legendary ebay seller called RaukCard ([youtube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9zDc3BltdawdWNazseoaxg) / [ebay](https://www.ebay.com/usr/raukcard)) has done a lot of work researching this set. Thanks to him we now know that these cards were distributed at McDonalds and came in 5 card paper booster packs. Below is his video opening one of these incredibly rare packs!
