Although the Digi-Battle card game wasn't too poplar back in the year 2000 there were people who loved the game and still collect the cards to this day! These are the most popular Digimon cards every year, determined by how views they had by the visitors of this website.

I've grouped the results by year and will continue to update the results each year for as long as I can.

*Update 10/07/2026: since removing the server-side of this website when updating, we no longer have page-level analytics. This means that this page won't be updated with stats for future years.*


## 2026

This is not an accurate reflection of 2026 because of the major site update, but here are the results up to the last database pull on 17/09/2026.

- In 1st place with **1909** views is [AeroVeedramon (BO-88)](/upper-deck/cards/aeroveedramon-bo-88)
- In 2nd place with **1734** views is [Puppetmon (BO-96)](/upper-deck/cards/puppetmon-bo-96)
- In 3rd place with **1660** views is [Agumon (ST-01)](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01)
- So far in 2026 the site had **628,629** page hits.


## 2025

[Omnimon (DM-01)](/upper-deck/cards/omnimon-dm-01) and Starter Set [Agumon](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01) traded places this year with Omnimon grabbing the win!

- [DM-01](/upper-deck/cards/omnimon-dm-01) was viewed **1783** times in 2025.
- In 2nd place with **1723** views this year was [Agumon (ST-01)](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01)
- In 3rd place with **1509** views was [MetalGreymon (DW-01)](/upper-deck/cards/metalgreymon-dw-01), making a return appearance to the top 3
- In 2025 Digi-Battle.com had **706,654** page hits.

2025 was full of good intentions and offline work, but ultimately nothing that went live. Improvements aside, this year has still been great and I want to say thanks again to everyone who came to the site or passed on messages!


## 2024

[Starter Set Agumon](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01) went from 3rd in 2022, to 2nd in 2023, and finally it's the 2024 card of the year! Happy to see the Digimon mascot himself getting lots of love this year.

- [ST-01](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01) was viewed **1477** times in 2024.
- In 2nd place with **1371** views this year was [Omnimon (DM-01)](/upper-deck/cards/omnimon-dm-01), creeping into the top 3 again.
- In 3rd place AGAIN with **1280** views this year was [AeroVeedramon (BO-88)](/upper-deck/cards/aeroveedramon-bo-88).
- In 2024 Digi-Battle.com had **554,619** page hits.

This year we hit 1 million overall page views! I say this every year, but I wish I'd had more time to devote to the site. I was definitely more productive than I was in 2023 but still plently more I'd like to do with the site. Thank you to everyone who's visited Digi-Battle.com and found it useful and a big thank you to everyone who contacted me or submitted cards to the [DW-01 archive](/dw01)! I hope to add more cool stuff in 2025.


## 2023

This year we have 2 Digimon finally managing to knock WarGreymon and MetalGreymon out of the top 3! I was surprised but not disappointed, [Machinedramon](/upper-deck/cards/machinedramon-bo-55) is an awesome Digimon with such a cool holographic card in the Digi-Battle card game. I'm happy to see this card getting some love in 2023.

- [BO-55](/upper-deck/cards/machinedramon-bo-55) was viewed **2326** times in 2023.
- In 2nd place with **1147** views this year was [Agumon (ST-01)](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01) creeping up to 2nd place from 3rd.
- In 3rd place with **1066** views this year was [AeroVeedramon (BO-88)](/upper-deck/cards/aeroveedramon-bo-88) finally making an appearance in the top 3.
- In 2023 Digi-Battle.com had **427,798** page hits.

I haven't had a lot of time to devote to working on the site this year but I hope I can change that next year. It makes me so happy to see that people are finding the information on here helpful. I'll keep on trying to make this website the best it can be. Let's hope there's more in 2024!


## 2022

It's another year in the top spot for fan-favorite [Wargreymon](/upper-deck/cards/wargreymon-tb-12)! No one should be surprised, it will always be an awesome card you can't help but look at.

- [TB-12](/upper-deck/cards/wargreymon-tb-12) was viewed **896** times in 2022.
- In 2nd place with **803** views this year was [MetalGreymon (DW-01)](/upper-deck/cards/metalgreymon-dw-01)&hellip; AGAIN!
- In 3rd place with **795** views this year was [Agumon (ST-01)](/upper-deck/cards/agumon-st-01) creeping into the top 3. Everyone loves Agumon!
- In 2022 Digi-Battle.com had **375,012** page hits.

I want to thank everyone again for coming to my site and enjoying it. I had another busy year adding the [DW-01 MetalGreymon Record Section](/dw01) and continuing to work throughout the site. I hope everyone finds the content useful and has as much fun as I do :)


## 2021

Shouldn't come as a surprise that [Wargreymon](/upper-deck/cards/wargreymon-tb-12) is the most popular card of 2021! This is a super cool card of one of the biggest fan favourites of the franchise so it's no wonder why it was viewed so much. TB-12 was viewed **503** times in 2021.

- In 2nd place with **446** views this year was [MetalGreymon (DW-01)](/upper-deck/cards/metalgreymon-dw-01) - this is one of my favourite cards so I imagine a lot of these page hits were me&hellip;
- In 3rd place with **430** views this year was [Omnimon (DM-01)](/upper-deck/cards/omnimon-dm-01).
- In 2021 Digi-Battle.com had **138,270** page hits.

I want to give a big THANK YOU to everyone who's used the site in 2021! All the feedback and comments sent have been greatly appreciated. I hope Digi-Battle.com can still be a useful tool for collectors in 2022 :)
