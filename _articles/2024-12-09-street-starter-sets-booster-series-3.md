I often get asked about the Street Starter sets and Booster Series 3 onwards and if I have any plans to add them to this site. So I've decided to write up a little article on the topic.

**tl;dr; I don't have them, go check out [websites that do](#OtherSites)**


## What's the Difference?

Upper Deck published the Digi-Battle card game from 1999 to the year 2000, only actually producing cards for around a year before Bandai took over publishing. Upper Deck released [10 sets and various promos](/upper-deck/sets) which you can find on this site.

Bandai went on to publish 4 more booster sets and 4 more starter decks. The booster sets continue on where Upper Deck left off starting from 3, which can cause some confusion as Upper Deck also released a [Booster Series 3](/upper-deck/sets/french-mega-pack) exclusively in France.

The big difference between the two is card design. When Bandai took over they redesigned the cards to have a new CGI look and feel.

Some people have a preference and others love them both, but personally the Upper Deck version is my favourite.

You won't find the Bandai cards on this website for a few reasons and it's not because I have anything against them:


### I don't have them

I'm not sure if Bandai even released their version of Digi-Battle in the UK. I definitely don't remember seeing any as a kid, so because of this I only had the Upper Deck cards.


### They're EXPENSIVE

I may be wrong but I don't think Bandai printed that many of each set, particularly towards the end, so these cards can be hard to come by. There's also holographic and gold text variants of (I think) every booster set card so there's even more cards to collect.

Add to that the fact that I live in the UK and shipping from the US can get very pricey very quickly. I don't see myself ever having the funds to complete the sets and add them.


### ~~It would break this site~~


### I don't have the time

There's so many cool things I'd like to do with this website that I just can't find the time for. I began work on this during the pandemic when free time was plenty, but now I'm wrapped up in family life and my career. I use what time I have to take small wins when I can.

I'm sorry to disappoint but I wouldn't hold out on seeing these cards here any time soon. But fear not!
