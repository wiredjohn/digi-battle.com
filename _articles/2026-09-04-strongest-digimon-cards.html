The card rank is calculated by summing the cards Red Attack power, Green Attack power and Yellow Attack power. In instances where two cards have the same overall power rating then they are sorted in alphabetical order.

This is by no means a perfect way of determining the best or worst cards in the game and doesn't take into account power option support, special effects, Digivolution lines or anything else. However this does give you a high level overview of what cards are likely to be better than others.

There's also a discrepency in the sort order when the cards are identical (e.g. Magnadramon CP-11 & BO-17). The overall rank is sorted descending by serial code and the level rank is sorted ascending, so one card will appear higher overall and the other will be higher in the level rank which doesn't make sense!
